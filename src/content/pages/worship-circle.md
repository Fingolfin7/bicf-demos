Worship Circle sessions is our worship community at BICF. It grew out of our worship choir weekends, which started back in 2016, and has developed into a place where we worship, grow, prepare and serve together.

## Who is it for?

- Singers and musicians
- Beginners who want to grow
- Experienced musicians who want to serve
- Anyone with a heart for worship

You do not have to be perfect. You need a willing heart and a desire to worship God and serve the church.

## What do we do?

Depending on the season and the events we are preparing for, our gatherings may include:

- Worship and prayer together
- Choir and vocal rehearsals: learning harmonies, blending voices and growing together as singers
- Band rehearsals: working on arrangements, dynamics, song structure and transitions
- Preparing for special events, such as Christmas, Easter, Worship Circle events and special Sundays
- Growing in musical skills and teamwork
- Encouraging one another spiritually and building our worship community

Not every gathering looks the same. Some are focused on preparation and rehearsal, while others give us more space to simply worship, connect and enjoy being together.

## How do we work?

Worship Circle sessions is an open community, but when we prepare for a specific event, we form a Serving Team of people who are able to commit to that event and its preparation.

- We meet regularly during the preparation season, usually every other week and sometimes on weekends, depending on the rehearsal schedule for the current event.
- We communicate through our private WhatsApp group, where we share important updates and stay connected as a team.
- For each event, we create a Serving Team. By joining the team, we commit to preparing and serving together for that particular event.
- We share materials in advance, such as harmony recordings, chords and lyrics, so everyone can prepare at home and we can use our rehearsal time well.

Our goal is not to become a professional or closed group. Worship is not only about singing or playing: it is about serving God, one another and our church well together.

## What is our goal?

To worship God together and help lead our church into worship. We want to be a community where people from different cultures, backgrounds and levels of musical experience can use their gifts, grow and serve together.

If you are interested in joining us, please contact Martin or Sarah. We would love to meet you!
