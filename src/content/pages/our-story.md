BICF began as a simple Bible study in a home many years ago. Over time the Bible study opened its doors to others, and it grew to include people from all over the world.

Today we are a member of the Evangelical Association in the Czech Republic, an affiliate of The Wesleyan Church, and part of the International Christian Community.
