## Where

We meet in the Majak Ministry Center, tucked away on the edge of Lesná in Brno.

The address is Dusíkova 5. The bus stop to look up if travelling by bus is Haškova. From there, take the path in front of the Billa store until you get to the yellow building. Then you've found us!

Let us say, it's easy once you know it, but the first time can be a little tricky.

## What to expect

We are an informal group, so come in whatever clothing is comfortable. And we tend to be friendly, so you will probably get to meet a few of us.

We invite you to participate in as much or as little as you would like. Typically we sing a few songs, share some of what's happening in the upcoming weeks, take time to pray, and look at the lessons for our lives that we can gain from the Bible.

Don't feel like you need to agree or be a Christian to come; we welcome any who would like to join our service.
