Our Sunday gathering is the centre of church life. It lasts about an hour and fifteen minutes, and most people stay afterwards for tea, coffee and something sweet.

## What happens

We sing together, often in English and occasionally in other languages our members bring with them. Someone reads from the Bible, and one of our speakers gives a talk that explains it and asks what it means for everyday life in Brno. We pray for each other, for the city and for the world.

## Who comes

Students on exchange, families who have lived in Brno for decades, researchers, Czech friends who want to speak English, people who are just curious. Many nationalities in one room, every week.

## Kids

Children start the service with everyone else and then head to their own programme during the talk. Parents are always welcome to stay with younger children.
