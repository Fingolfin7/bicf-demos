This website does not use cookies, analytics or any tracking. Fonts and images are served from our own site, so visiting it tells no one else that you were here.

## Forms

When you send a message through the contact form, we receive your name, email address and message so that we can reply. We delete messages after twelve months.

Prayer requests are emailed straight to our prayer team and are never stored on the website.

## Sign-up sheets

When you sign up for something, we keep your name and email address until the event is over, so the organiser can contact you. The sign-up sheet on the website shows how many places are taken, never who took them.

## Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete anything we hold about you. Write to we-are@bicf.cz. The full policy is on our [organizational information](../organizational-information/) page.
