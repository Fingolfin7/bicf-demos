Brno International Christian Fellowship is a registered congregation of the Evangelical Association in the Czech Republic.

## Leadership

The church is led by a pastor and an elected leadership team. Members vote on the leadership team at the annual meeting in January.

## Giving

BICF is supported entirely by the gifts of the people who attend. Account details are available from the treasurer on request.
