Brno International Christian Fellowship, z. s. is a non-profit organisation (IČ: 06243185) and operates the website www.bicf.cz. If you are a member, take part in our events, subscribe to our newsletter or visit the website, you are giving us your personal information. We are responsible for its protection and security.

You can contact us about the processing of your information at any time at [we-are@bicf.cz](mailto:we-are@bicf.cz).

## What we process and why

We process personal data you entrust to us for the provision of services, performance of contracts, keeping of accounts and sending newsletters. We only send you newsletters with your consent, and you can withdraw it with the unsubscribe link in every email.

We keep your personal information for the legal limitation period, unless the law requires a longer period or we have said otherwise in a specific case.

## Who can see it

Our employees, members of the association and authorised external accountants may have access to your personal data. All processing happens within the EU. Without your consent, your personal data will not be released to any third party.

## Your rights

- **Access:** you can ask what we hold about you, and we will reply within 30 days.
- **Correction:** if anything is outdated or incomplete, you can have it changed.
- **Restriction:** you can limit how we process your data if you think it is incorrect or unlawful.
- **Portability:** we will send your data in a machine-readable form within 30 days.
- **Deletion:** we will delete your data from our systems and backups within 20 days, except where the law requires us to keep it, for example accounting documents.
- **Complaint:** you can complain to the Office for Personal Data Protection at any time, though we would be glad if you told us first so we can put things right.

This privacy policy applies from 25 May 2018.
