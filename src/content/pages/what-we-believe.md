We come from many church traditions, and some of us from none at all. What holds us together is a shared trust in Jesus Christ.

## God

We believe in one God, Father, Son and Holy Spirit, who made the world and loves it.

## Jesus

We believe that Jesus Christ lived, died and rose again, and that through him anyone can be forgiven and brought home to God.

## The Bible

We believe the Bible is God's word, and we read it together to learn how to follow him.

## The church

We believe the church is a family drawn from every nation, and that it exists to worship God, care for one another and serve the city.
