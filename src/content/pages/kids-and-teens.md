During the Sunday talk, children aged 3 to 12 go to their own room with two of our trained leaders. They hear the same Bible passage as the adults, told in a way that makes sense to them, with games, crafts and a lot of questions.

Teens meet once a month on a Saturday afternoon for food, games and an honest conversation about faith.

## Keeping children safe

Every leader working with children has agreed to our child safety policy. Parents sign their children in and collect them in person.
