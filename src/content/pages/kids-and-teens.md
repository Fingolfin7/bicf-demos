## Children's ministry

Our children's ministry has a weekly ministry time for children ages 4 to 15. Our teaching team seeks to creatively teach our kids and teens Biblical truth and the power of the gospel through engaging lessons and activities on Sunday evenings.

We also have a number of special holiday programmes during the year which involve our children, as well as unique ministry events and activities.

## Teens for Christ

TFC (Teens for Christ) is a weekly ministry geared for teenagers ages 12 and up. This is a time of focused Bible study, games, prayer, food and fun! Our goal is to equip teens to put Christ first in their lives and learn to share their faith with others.

TFC meets on Fridays from 16:00 to 18:00.

## Toddlers Sunday School

Our Toddlers Sunday School is a special time for children ages 1 to 4 to learn about Jesus through Bible stories, fun games, songs, and hands-on activities. Everything is designed so they can learn while they play, in a loving and safe environment.

## Nursery for babies

We also have a cosy nursery where parents are welcome to stay with their little ones while still participating in the service.
