Thanks for taking a moment to let us greet you and share a little about who we are and why we do the things we do.

We are a group of people who have come from near and far. We are an international mix of nationalities. We speak English in our gatherings, but love to hear many languages being used in times of fellowship. We are an open group and invite anyone and everyone to join us.

Our desire in all we do is to seek for God and his will and then be obedient to follow Him. We see that happening best when we're together, so we like to get together often and in a number of ways.

## Not sure about God?

If you're unsure about God, Jesus, the Bible, etc., that's ok. You're as welcome as anyone. And we hope that you'll consider checking out God and the church with our group.

There's no requirement to agree with a certain set of beliefs to come and join us. Truth is, we probably don't agree on everything in our group already. The goal isn't conformity. The desire is to know more of the God who made us, and we'll never be done with that quest in this life.
