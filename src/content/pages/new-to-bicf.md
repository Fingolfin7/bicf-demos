We are an open group and invite anyone and everyone to join us. There's no requirement to agree with a certain set of beliefs to come along. Our aim is to seek God's will together.

Meetings are in English, though you'll hear many other languages over tea afterwards. Finding us for the first time can be a little tricky, so look at the directions, or send us a message and someone will meet you at the tram stop.
