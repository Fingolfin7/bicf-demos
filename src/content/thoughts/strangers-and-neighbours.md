Almost everyone at BICF has, at some point, been the new person in a strange city. We remember the first trip to the cizinecká policie, the first tram ticket bought in the wrong zone, the first Sunday alone.

That shared memory is a gift. It means we know exactly what it feels like to walk into a room full of strangers, and we know how much a simple "Where are you from?" can mean.

## Hospitality is a habit

Hebrews tells us not to forget to show hospitality to strangers. The word it uses, *philoxenia*, literally means love of the stranger. It's the opposite of the fear of outsiders that comes so naturally to all of us.

This week, look out for the person standing alone with a cup of tea. You were them once.
