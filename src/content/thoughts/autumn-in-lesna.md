Every autumn the woods of Čertova rokle, just behind our Sunday home, let go of everything. The trees don't panic about it. They have done it before, and spring has always come.

Many of us are in a season of letting go: of a country, a language, a job, a plan we had for our lives. Ecclesiastes reminds us that there is a time for everything, and that the seasons are held in hands bigger than ours.
